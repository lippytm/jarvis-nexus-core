## Purpose

Build a zero-ad-spend marketing preparation layer that connects reviewed products and projects to free organic distribution markets without pretending that account connections, publication, traffic, leads, sales or commissions already exist.

## Product -> Campaign -> Newsletter pipeline

`Product Record -> Campaign Packet -> 14 Channel Briefs -> Prompt #11 Review -> Newsletter Candidate -> Platform-Specific Draft -> Human Approval -> Publish (future authorized action) -> Metrics`

The compiler currently stops before publication.

## Claim controls

Funding customer campaigns must not promise:
- approval;
- financing amount;
- interest rate;
- credit result.

Referral-partner campaigns must not promise:
- earnings;
- commissions;
- automatic income;
- business results.

Publishing campaigns must not claim an edition is published, minted, sold or available until the exact edition and destination are verified.

AI/Jarvis campaigns must distinguish tested repository artifacts from planned integrations, production deployment and autonomous background operation.

## Channel adaptation rules

- **LinkedIn:** professional project/business framing.
- **YouTube:** tutorial, documentary, trailer or educational video framing.
- **Facebook:** useful explanation, community question and relevant next action.
- **Instagram:** Reel/carousel with visual-first concept and accessible captioning.
- **Substack:** long-form newsletter/article derivative.
- **Reddit:** discussion-first; community self-promotion/link rules control.
- **Pinterest:** visual concepts, diagrams, book/wearable/robotics imagery.
- **TikTok:** short educational/entertainment video.
- **GitHub:** engineering proof, docs, code, tests and reproducibility.
- **Threads:** concise development discussion.
- **Google Business / Bing Places / Apple Business Connect / Nextdoor:** only after exact business identity, eligibility and listing destination are verified.

## Measurement model

Keep these distinct:

`DRAFT -> REVIEWED -> PUBLISHED -> IMPRESSION -> CLICK -> SUBSCRIBER -> CONSENTED LEAD -> SALE -> VERIFIED COMMISSION -> COST -> CONTRIBUTION`

No earlier state implies a later one.

## Prompt #11 marketing gate

Every campaign asks:

1. Is the product/destination real and correctly identified?
2. Are factual claims supported?
3. Is the audience appropriate?
4. Are affiliate/referral disclosures present where needed?
5. Are financing and earnings guarantees excluded?
6. Is private/internal information excluded?
7. Is the channel format native to that market?
8. Is the CTA accurate for the journey?
9. Is the destination tagged without corrupting existing referral parameters?
10. Is actual performance measured separately from generated copy?
