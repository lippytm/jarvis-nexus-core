## Prompt #11 rules

1. Fiction/comedy/satire are visibly separated from factual claims.
2. Jokes do not become evidence.
3. Satire does not imply verified political, military, financial or technical outcomes.
4. Funding copy never promises approval, rate, amount or credit outcome.
5. Partner copy never promises earnings or commissions.
6. Unreleased books do not receive invented purchase destinations.
7. No invented testimonials, audience size, revenue or sales.
8. TikTok remains blocked for this owner.
9. No publication occurs without authorized account access and human review.
