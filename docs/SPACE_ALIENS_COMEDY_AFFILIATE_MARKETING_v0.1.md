## Purpose

Create a reusable comedy-affiliate overlay for every item that is **currently registered** in the Jarvis Control Tower, while keeping real product facts, affiliate terms, membership states, destinations, disclosures and evidence separate from the fictional/ANIMA layer.

## Master hook

**Join the Space Aliens in the Affiliate Marketing Universes of Life/Business Building.**

## Operating cycle

**Entertain -> Teach -> Bridge -> Disclose -> Route -> Measure -> Improve -> Evolve**

Comedy gets attention. Education earns the next moment. One relevant business route provides the optional next step. Prompt #11 keeps the claim boundary intact.

## Five comedy engines

1. **Space Alien Misunderstanding** - aliens misunderstand human business or technology, then the punchline reveals something useful.
2. **Intergalactic Bureaucracy** - absurd fictional departments and paperwork expose a real process problem.
3. **Pun Bridge** - a clean pun opens the door, one useful lesson follows, then one optional business route.
4. **Roast & Rebuild** - roast a poor idea or workflow, then rebuild it with a better documented alternative.
5. **Comedy Quest** - convert a useful next action into an ANIMA/RPG quest with an evidence checkpoint.

## Product-to-route mapping

Each registered product gets **one primary route per derivative**:

- Education Hub -> education membership.
- GetBizFunds funding information -> funding information route.
- 7 Figures Credit funding information -> funding information route.
- 7 Figures partner -> Affiliate Builder membership route.
- LendTrack partner -> referral-partner route.
- Book/Chapter Incubator -> Book/ANIMA route.
- AI Building AI / Jarvis Nexus -> Jarvis Builders route.

This prevents comedy copy from mixing financing customers, partner recruitment, membership concepts and technical products into one confusing CTA.

## Prompt #11 boundary

- Fiction/comedy/satire are never evidence.
- Use one primary route per derivative.
- Verify exact offers, destinations and terms before publication.
- Preserve affiliate/referral disclosures.
- Never guarantee approval, income, commissions, sales, demand or business outcomes.
- Never invent publication status, testimonials, member counts, traffic, conversions or revenue.
- Membership concepts do not become paid offers until pricing, fulfillment and destination are verified.
- Live publication requires authorized account access and human release review.
