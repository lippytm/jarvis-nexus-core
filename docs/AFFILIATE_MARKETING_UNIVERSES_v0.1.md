## Concept

The affiliate system is not one funnel. It is a collection of opportunity worlds that can connect education, entertainment, products, memberships and verified third-party programs.

"Unlimited possibilities" and "infinite opportunities" are creative horizon language. They do not mean unlimited real affiliate programs, guaranteed acceptance, guaranteed sales, guaranteed commissions or guaranteed income.

The operating cycle is:

**Discover -> Verify -> Educate -> Disclose -> Route -> Measure -> Improve**

## Twelve opportunity worlds

1. **Business Funding & Referral Universe** - financing education and separately disclosed referral-partner routes.
2. **AI Tools & Software Universe** - reviews, tutorials and future verified software affiliations.
3. **Hosting / Websites / Domains Universe** - build tutorials plus verified hosting/domain referrals.
4. **Education & Membership Universe** - Free Explorer, Builder Member, Affiliate Builder and Business Creator concepts.
5. **Books / Media / Publishing Universe** - eBooks, audiobooks, video books, ANIMA and newsletters.
6. **Robotics & Wearable Technology Universe** - robotics/humanoids and CryptoCyber wearable families.
7. **3D Printing & Manufacturing Universe** - 3D printable wearables, robotics components and maker education.
8. **Developer / Linux / Blockchain Universe** - coding, Bedrock, OMARCHY, blockchain and developer tools.
9. **Creator Studio & Multimedia Universe** - video, audio, animation, documentaries, trailers and ad-copy services.
10. **Newsletter & Community Universe** - recurring audience, comedy, education and future sponsorship/affiliate routes.
11. **Local Business Services Universe** - websites, CRM, automation, local business education and services.
12. **Space Alien Entertainment Universe** - fiction, comedy, satire, ANIMA/RPG and wordplay that can introduce one real-world route.

## Route classes

Every opportunity must identify itself as one of:

- **Owned Product**
- **Affiliate / Referral**
- **Membership**
- **Education**
- **Research**
- **Fiction / Entertainment**

This prevents a planned product, a research concept and a live third-party referral from being presented as though they are the same thing.

## Prompt #11 Affiliate Gate

Before any opportunity becomes a campaign:

1. Verify the provider or owned offer.
2. Verify the exact destination.
3. Verify current terms and eligibility.
4. Verify what can legally and accurately be claimed.
5. Add affiliate/referral disclosure where applicable.
6. Separate financing customers from referral-partner recruitment.
7. Never guarantee income, commissions, approval or demand.
8. Track clicks, leads, sales and commissions separately.
9. Preserve the fictional/ANIMA label where used.
10. Scale only after evidenced results.
