# Prompt #11 — Quality & Quality Assurance Standard v0.1

**Owner:** Intergalactic Corporate Services LLC / lippytm.AI  
**Program:** Evolutionary Evolutions — AI Building AI with AI Jarvis Assistants Swarms Systems Communications Networks  
**Priority:** JOB #1 — Quality and Quality Assurance  
**Status:** Architecture and governance standard  
**Date:** 2026-10-26

## 1. Governing rule

**Quality and Quality Assurance are Job #1.**

Prompt #11 is the umbrella QA constitution for the Business of Businesses / Systems of Systems architecture. Every AI Jarvis capability, Skill, swarm workflow, trading bot, AI sandbox, Linux/Bedrock/OMARCHY exercise, robot, wearable, website, book, multimedia derivative, business workflow and release must be designed so that quality can be specified, tested, evidenced, reviewed and improved.

Nothing is promoted merely because it was generated.

## 2. Prompt #11 quality doctrine

Every mission follows:

**Specify -> Build -> Test -> Evaluate -> Challenge -> Document -> Review -> Release -> Measure -> Improve**

A successful generation is not automatically a successful product, lesson, agent, trade strategy, robot behavior or business outcome.

## 3. Required quality gates

### Q0 — Mission Quality
- exact objective;
- owner/project;
- permitted scope;
- acceptance criteria;
- stop conditions;
- risk tier;
- evidence requirements.

### Q1 — Source Quality
- authoritative/relevant sources where applicable;
- source revision/date;
- attribution;
- uncertainty;
- contradictions retained;
- fiction separated from factual claims.

### Q2 — Design Quality
- smallest useful architecture;
- explicit interfaces;
- least privilege;
- sandbox boundary;
- rollback/recovery;
- accessibility and usability requirements;
- failure modes identified.

### Q3 — Build Quality
- versioned source;
- pinned/controlled dependencies where practical;
- no secrets in source/prompts/logs;
- reproducible build instructions;
- changed files recorded.

### Q4 — Verification Quality
- syntax/static checks where applicable;
- meaningful behavior tests;
- negative/error-path tests;
- adversarial and prompt-injection tests for AI workflows;
- isolation tests for bots/agents;
- regression checks;
- observed failures retained.

### Q5 — Independent Evaluation
- Builder does not self-certify high-risk work;
- evaluator attempts to disprove success;
- compare results with acceptance criteria;
- distinguish simulated, tested-local and real-world evidence.

### Q6 — Safety & Security
- identity and permissions verified;
- tool risk checked;
- no unauthorized cross-agent access;
- credentials revocable;
- kill/stop mechanism tested for sensitive systems;
- financial and physical actions remain separately gated.

### Q7 — Documentation & Provenance
- evidence record;
- source/commit/artifact hashes where useful;
- test results;
- limitations;
- reviewer state;
- release decision;
- next task.

### Q8 — Release Quality
- human/release authority identified;
- correct audience/destination;
- privacy classification checked;
- rollback/recovery available;
- publication/deployment/trade/physical action explicitly authorized.

### Q9 — Outcome Quality
Measure what happened after release:
- defects/incidents;
- usability/accessibility;
- learner transfer;
- customer/reader engagement;
- revenue/cost where relevant;
- support load;
- risk events;
- improvement opportunities.

## 4. Quality statuses

Use explicit states:

- `PLANNED`
- `DRAFT`
- `SIMULATED`
- `TESTED_LOCAL`
- `REVIEWED`
- `BLOCKED`
- `APPROVED_FOR_NEXT_GATE`
- `RELEASED`
- `OBSERVED_IN_PRODUCTION`
- `RETIRED`

Never collapse these into a vague claim that something is "done."

## 5. AI Building AI quality rule

AI may propose:
- new prompts;
- Skills;
- tools;
- tests;
- architectures;
- agents;
- patches;
- learning content;
- production derivatives.

AI may not silently activate its own proposed capability.

A new AI-built capability must pass Prompt #11 quality gates before promotion.

## 6. Swarm quality architecture

Specialized roles:

- **Planner** — requirements and acceptance.
- **Researcher** — sources and uncertainty.
- **Architect** — design and failure modes.
- **Builder** — bounded implementation.
- **Security Reviewer** — permissions, tool/sandbox and injection risks.
- **Evaluator** — behavior and counterexamples.
- **Evidence Reporter** — provenance and limitations.
- **Release Owner** — final authorization.

High-risk workflows separate Builder, Evaluator and Release Owner.

## 7. Trading-bot QA

Every individual trading bot remains a separate system with a separate AI Sandbox.

Prompt #11 requires each bot to independently pass:
- strategy specification;
- data-quality review;
- backtest reproducibility;
- leakage/look-ahead checks;
- out-of-sample/walk-forward evaluation;
- deterministic pre-trade risk checks;
- paper execution;
- kill-switch test;
- cross-bot isolation test;
- evidence logging;
- human review.

Backtest, paper and any future live results are never mixed.

Historical or simulated profit is not proof of future performance.

## 8. Robotics QA

Robotics progression:

**Simulation -> Safety Review -> Hardware-in-the-Loop -> Supervised Trial -> Observed Verification -> Broader Permission**

Prompt #11 requires:
- independent emergency stop;
- authenticated command path;
- safe-state behavior;
- telemetry;
- incident log;
- human authorization for higher-risk physical action.

Simulation success is not physical readiness.

## 9. Wearable QA

Wearables require:
- device identity;
- secure pairing;
- sensor permission review;
- visible recording states;
- accessibility review;
- privacy/retention settings;
- signed/validated update path;
- failure/offline mode;
- human-readable safety status.

Do not make health, safety or security claims without appropriate evidence.

## 10. Linux / Bedrock / OMARCHY QA

Every system-level exercise uses:
- recoverable environment;
- snapshot/rollback;
- explicit distro/stratum ownership;
- configuration under version control;
- no production credentials in learning labs;
- reproducible commands;
- expected observations;
- transfer assessment.

Bedrock/OMARCHY experiments graduate only after observed compatibility evidence.

## 11. Educational QA

Each lesson/quest records:
- objective;
- prerequisites;
- expected observable result;
- bounded practice;
- hints;
- authored checks where appropriate;
- transfer task;
- learner explanation/reflection;
- evidence artifact.

Correct answer matching alone does not establish mastery.

## 12. 19-book / multimedia QA

All 19 books are expressed through all 19 objectives, while each book keeps a dominant objective.

Every derivative — NFT/collector, eBook, audiobook, video book, ANIMA RPG, interactive lesson, trailer, newsletter, ad or AR/robotics adaptation — carries:
- source revision;
- canon/factual classification;
- evidence references;
- rights/license state;
- accessibility requirements;
- reviewer state;
- version/hash where useful.

The recurring Quantum Questions Entangled and entertainment chapters share structure but must have different book-specific content.

## 13. Documentary / fiction QA

Never silently mix:
- verified documentary facts;
- attributed claims;
- unresolved questions;
- owner-reported information;
- fictionalization;
- satire/comedy;
- educational simulation.

Prompt #11 requires visible classification when these lanes meet.

## 14. Business & revenue QA

Separate:
- generated content;
- published content;
- reach;
- qualified leads;
- conversions;
- paid revenue;
- verified commissions;
- costs;
- refunds;
- support load.

Generated advertising is not revenue.

## 15. Prompt #11 QA scorecard

Every major release should answer:

1. What was the exact objective?
2. What changed?
3. What evidence supports it?
4. What tests actually ran?
5. What failed?
6. What remains unverified?
7. What permissions were used?
8. What could cause harm or loss?
9. Can it be stopped or rolled back?
10. Who reviewed it?
11. Who authorized release?
12. What measurable outcome followed?
13. What is the next improvement?

## 16. Quality escalation

When a defect is found:

**STOP -> ISOLATE -> PRESERVE EVIDENCE -> DIAGNOSE -> CORRECT -> RETEST -> INDEPENDENT REVIEW -> RESUME OR RETIRE**

The defect becomes a new bounded mission.

## 17. Evolutionary Evolutions quality loop

The permanent loop is:

**Build -> Test -> Evaluate -> Document -> Communicate -> Improve -> Evolve**

But the governing condition is:

> **No Evolution without Evidence. No Expansion without Quality Assurance.**

## 18. Acceptance standard

A capability can be called integrated only when:
- its identity is unique;
- scope and permissions are explicit;
- sandbox/risk boundary is documented;
- meaningful tests pass;
- failures and limitations are recorded;
- evidence is retained;
- release authority is explicit;
- rollback/recovery exists;
- actual outcomes are distinguished from expected benefits.

## Closing principle

**Prompt #11: Quality and Quality Assurance are Job #1.**

Quality is not the final inspection step. It is the architecture through which AI Building AI, AI Jarvis Assistants, Swarms Systems and Communications Networks are allowed to evolve.
